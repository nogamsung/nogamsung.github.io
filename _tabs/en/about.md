Hi, I'm **Geomseong Ahn**, a product engineer based in Korea.

This blog is where I write down the problems I run into while building and running software, the calls I made, and how they turned out.

## What I do

From 2023 to 2025 I worked on Spring Boot backends at a travel platform company.
The early posts here (JPA and Querydsl performance work, Redis distributed locks, rolling out Kong as an API gateway) come from that time.

Since 2025 I've been a product engineer at a manufacturing company.
I design our internal platform from scratch and own it all the way through deployment and operations.
The recent posts are a record of the decisions I've made along the way, and why.

## What I write about

- **Backend**: Spring Boot and JPA performance, concurrency control, circuit breakers, module structure
- **Auth and security**: JWT session design, making permission changes take effect immediately, cookies and BFFs
- **Frontend and real-time**: running Next.js in production, WebSocket reconnects, unread counts
- **Infrastructure and deployment**: zero-downtime deploys on bare metal with Kamal, API gateways, containers
- **AI-native development**: adopting coding agents and building a harness for them, LLMs inside the product

## How I write

- **Start with the problem.** What broke, and why it mattered.
- **Show the reasoning.** Which options I compared and why I picked the one I did.
- **Make it reproducible.** The actual code and config I shipped, plus the numbers I measured.

These are the decisions I made at the time, not the one right answer. If you know a better way, I'd love to hear it.
